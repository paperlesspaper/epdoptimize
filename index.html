EXPERIMENTAL TESTS — Spectra35

35 recipe colors for the tested OpenPaper L, calibrated relative to aitjcize Spectra6. This is not a standard panel mode. Color accuracy, stability and panel lifetime are not validated; custom waveforms can damage the panel.

The dithered image uses experimental matching RGB estimates; extrapolated intermediate colors remain uncertain. The almost-black token PNG requires custom firmware and the multi-pass LUT planner. Do not upload it to ordinary Spectra6 firmware.

For the tested planner, choose OpenPaper L at 1600 × 1200 in landscape before processing. Keep the token PNG lossless and unmodified.
